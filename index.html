Before your first session

GOOD QUESTIONS.

I'm a beginner. Can I join?

Yes. The studio concept is built around coached sessions with movement options for different levels. A real studio would discuss your experience and any relevant limitations before training.

What should I bring?

Comfortable training clothes, suitable shoes, a water bottle and a small towel. Aim to arrive a little early to settle in.

Does selecting a session book a place?

No. This is a portfolio demo. Selecting a session fills the sample enquiry form, but does not reserve a place, take payment or send your details.
